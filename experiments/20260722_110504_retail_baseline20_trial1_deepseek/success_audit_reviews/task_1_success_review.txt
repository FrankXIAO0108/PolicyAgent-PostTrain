=== POLICY FAILURES ===

1. MULTI_TOOL_CALL_POLICY_VIOLATION

The frozen Retail policy states:

"You should at most make one tool call at a time"

The Agent makes two tool calls in one assistant turn on two occasions:

- get_order_details + get_user_details
- get_product_details(keyboard) + get_product_details(thermostat)

The calls are individually reasonable, but their grouping violates the explicit trajectory-level policy.

2. TEXT_AND_TOOL_CALL_POLICY_VIOLATION

The frozen Retail policy states:

"if you take a tool call, you should not respond to the user at the same time"

Every tool-calling assistant message also contains user-facing text, including:

- "Let me look you up with your name and zip code."
- "Let me start by checking the order details and your user information."
- "Great! ... Let me look up the product details..."
- "Let me process the exchange now."

The official evaluator and static action checks do not expose this violation.

3. UNSUPPORTED_PROCEDURAL_CLAIM

The final response claims:

"Refund of $13.46 will go back to your Mastercard ending in 2478
(within 5-7 business days since it's a credit card)"

The frozen Exchange policy specifies the payment method and price-difference handling, but does not specify a
5-to-7-business-day refund timeline for exchanges. That timing exists elsewhere in the policy for cancellation and
some modification flows. Reusing it here is an unsupported procedural claim.

The final response also says the user will receive an email "shortly." The policy supports that an email regarding
how to return items will be sent, but it does not support the added timing word "shortly."
