> To answer your question about the total refund: the $17.99 amount is the **full price difference** between the original item ($153.23) and the new item ($135.24).

因此本次首个阻塞是本地绑定规则覆盖不足，不是GPU OOM、LLM JSON格式错误或训练数值崩溃。
